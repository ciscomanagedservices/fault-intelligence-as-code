# Comparisons

## What Belongs Here

**Purpose:** Side-by-side analyses of tools, vendors, protocols, or approaches — decision support material for architecture and operational choices.

**Types of content that belong here:**
- Vendor comparisons (Cisco vs. Juniper for a specific use case)
- Protocol comparisons (OSPF vs. BGP for internal routing)
- Tool comparisons (SolarWinds vs. LibreNMS for monitoring)
- Architecture pattern comparisons (hub-and-spoke vs. full mesh)
- Platform evaluations done before a purchasing or design decision

**Boundary:** Comparisons are *analytical*, not procedural. The outcome of a comparison (the decision made) belongs in `decisions/`. The technical concepts being compared are defined in `concepts/`. This folder is for the structured side-by-side analysis itself.
