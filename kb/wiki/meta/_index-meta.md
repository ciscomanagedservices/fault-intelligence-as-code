# Meta

## What Belongs Here

**Purpose:** Wiki health, dashboards, lint reports, conventions, and operational metadata about the wiki itself.

**Types of content that belong here:**
- Lint reports (orphan pages, dead links, stale content)
- Dataview dashboards
- Wiki conventions and style guides
- Folder and naming conventions
- Coverage maps (what's well-documented vs. sparse)

**Boundary:** This folder is about the *wiki as a system*, not about the network. No network content belongs here.
